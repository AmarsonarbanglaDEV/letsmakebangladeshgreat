# Data Governance, Evidence and Publication Policy

## 1. Core rule

A public record must be traceable to an identified source and must not claim more than that source establishes.

## 2. Evidence states

Use separate states:
- Collected: source received, not yet checked.
- Extracted: fields transcribed or parsed.
- Reviewed: values checked against the cited source.
- Corroborated: independently supported by an additional suitable source.
- Approved: owner/editorial review completed.
- Published: visible to the public.
- Withdrawn: removed or restricted due to a source, rights, privacy or accuracy issue.

Do not use "verified" without defining what was checked.

## 3. Financial distinctions

Never conflate:
- Budget allocation
- Revised allocation
- Fund release
- Transfer
- Contract award
- Invoice or payment
- Reported expenditure
- Audited expenditure

A transfer to a division, ministry or agency does not establish spending on a specific project. A contract award is not proof that the entire contract value has been paid. A cumulative report is not a new transaction.

Store amounts as exact decimal strings or database NUMERIC values, with the original currency, scale and unit. Never silently convert currencies or units.

## 4. Project status rules

A project may be labelled delayed only against a documented schedule and a dated progress source.

Use cancelled, terminated, suspended, discontinued and abandoned only when source documents support the distinction. Absence of an update is "no recent public update" or "status unconfirmed", not "abandoned".

Official completion and independent physical verification must be separate fields.

## 5. Audit and allegations

An audit observation is not automatically proof of fraud, corruption or criminal wrongdoing. Publish the audit institution, report, page, amount, response and follow-up status. Use restrained factual language and provide correction routes.

Do not publish unsubstantiated allegations about individuals.

## 6. Environmental commitments

Keep reported planting, documented planting sites, independent planting checks, survival checks and replanting separate. Specify species, count, date, location and responsible programme when available. Never translate saplings reportedly planted into trees surviving without follow-up evidence.

## 7. International indicators

For each indicator preserve:
- Indicator definition
- Value and unit
- Reference period
- Publication date
- Source institution and URL
- Methodology
- Geographic/population coverage
- Observed / estimated / projected status
- Country rank and comparison universe, if supplied by the source

Do not combine incompatible years or methods into a single league table.

## 8. Sources and preservation

Record source institution, title, original URL, access date, publication date, page/table, version, checksum, usage rights and review notes.

Keep source originals private unless their licence and privacy status permit publication. Publicly linking to an official source is preferred to rehosting a restricted document.

When a source is revoked or corrected, identify affected records and withdraw or update linked public snapshots with a visible history. Never silently restore withdrawn content.

## 9. Privacy and submissions

Do not collect unnecessary personal information. Do not request confidential documents or identify whistleblowers through public forms. A private reporting system must be designed, secured, consented and reviewed before launch.

Saving an observation or generating a draft is not filing an official complaint. Do not imply that a provider email receipt equals official registration.

## 10. Publication gate

Public release is blocked until:
1. Source identity and rights are checked.
2. Data extraction is reviewed.
3. Units, fiscal year and accounting basis are recorded.
4. Duplicate and conflicting records are checked.
5. Project identity and geographic linkage are supported.
6. Claims are written proportionately to evidence.
7. Privacy and security checks pass.
8. Corrections and change history are available.
9. An authorized reviewer approves publication.

## 11. Corrections

A correction must record the affected public record, issue, supporting material, decision, date and resulting revision. Do not delete the historical correction trail.
