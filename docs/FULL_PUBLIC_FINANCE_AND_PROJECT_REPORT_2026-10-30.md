# Full Public-Finance and Development Atlas Report

**Project:** Let's Make Bangladesh Great  
**Report snapshot:** 30 October 2026  
**Data currency:** Bangladeshi taka (BDT)  
**Money unit in national budget tables:** BDT crore, unless stated otherwise  
**Editorial status:** Research/pilot report. Not a complete national expenditure audit.

---

## 1. Executive summary

This report establishes a source-linked first dataset for the Bangladesh public-finance and development atlas. It combines the FY2026–27 national budget speech, the current ADP implementation release, the Road Transport and Highways Division (RTHD) approved-project register, the RTHD mega-project index, the Bangladesh Public Procurement Authority (BPPA) contract-award register and the Bangladesh National Portal's administrative directory.

The main findings in this snapshot are:

- The FY2026–27 national budget speech proposes **Tk 9,38,000 crore** in total expenditure, against revenue income of **Tk 6,95,000 crore** and a stated budget deficit of **Tk 2,43,000 crore**.
- The budget speech's Table 2 includes **Tk 3,00,000 crore** for the Annual Development Programme (ADP). A separate IMED-reported first-quarter implementation calculation uses **Tk 3,08,924.86 crore** as its programme allocation denominator. These two published bases are recorded separately pending reconciliation.
- The Ministry of Defence line is **Tk 42,497 crore** and the Armed Forces Division line is **Tk 44 crore**. Their arithmetic sum is **Tk 42,541 crore**, about **4.54%** of the national budget. The budget speech does not provide a defence-specific expenditure/outcome table that allows the allocation to be converted into a claim about actual capability or outputs.
- The official RTHD approved-project page reports **73 entries**. This pilot transcribes the **10 entries displayed on page 1**; a separate mega-project record covers MRT Line-6. This is not the full RTHD register and is not a nationwide all-ministry project inventory.
- Six road-related BPPA contract-award examples are included. Four have been cross-checked against their individual BPPA award-detail pages. They do not represent all road contracts or all road contractors.
- The national portal lists **8 divisions and 64 districts**. All 64 district names are in the administrative directory. Project pins are not fabricated: where an official record gives only a district or an administrative project area, the map should highlight the relevant area rather than invent an exact GPS point.

**Crucial financial distinction:** Budget allocation, revised allocation, fund release, contract award, invoice/payment, reported expenditure and audited expenditure are different events. This atlas keeps them separate.

### 2.4 Defence allocation and outcomes

- Ministry of Defence allocation: **Tk 42,497 crore**.
- Armed Forces Division allocation: **Tk 44 crore**.
- Arithmetic total of these two separate lines: **Tk 42,541 crore**, around **4.54%** of the Tk 938,000 crore national budget.

The two line items must remain separate in the raw dataset. The combined amount is a simple arithmetic sum for a reader's overview, not a separately published portfolio total. The budget speech Table 4 does not list the Ministry of Defence as a separate line with an FY2024–25 actual. The General Services actual cannot be substituted for a defence actual because it covers a wider set of activities.

**What is the expected outcome?** A budget allocation alone does not specify procurement delivery, force readiness, operational capability, service outputs or measurable public outcomes. A defensible defence-outcome panel needs the relevant approved programme/modernisation documents, procurement notices and awards where public, delivery/acceptance records, audited expenditure and published performance information. No defence outcome score is generated from this budget table.

## 3. ADP implementation — latest reported national snapshot

IMED's report published **25 October 2026** states that FY2026–27 ADP financial implementation through July–September 2026 was **3.89%**. UNB's 25 October report, citing IMED, gives expenditure of **Tk 12,017.54 crore** against an implementation denominator of **Tk 308,924.86 crore**. This is a national aggregate; it is not the progress of each individual project.

The budget speech's Table 2 lists ADP at Tk 300,000 crore. The 308,924.86 crore denominator reported for the current IMED implementation calculation is not identical. The atlas preserves both numbers and flags the discrepancy for reconciliation; it does not silently replace one with the other.

## 4. Mega-project model and project progress

### 4.1 Dhaka Mass Rapid Transit Development Project — MRT Line-6

**Implementing agency:** Dhaka Mass Transit Company Limited (DMTCL)  
**Responsible ministry/sector:** Road Transport and Bridges  
**Published corridor description:** Uttara North–Motijheel, with a Motijheel–Kamalapur extension  
**Map precision:** Dhaka district/corridor context only in this pilot; no official route line, surveyed station geometry or project-specific coordinates have been imported.  
**3D model:** The website should show a labelled *conceptual elevated-metro schematic*. It is not the official design, BIM model, engineering geometry, or as-built record.

RTHD's progress PDF dated **28 February 2026** provides the following values:

| Measure | Reported progress | Scope |
|---|---:|---|
| Physical progress | 99.60% | Uttara North–Motijheel section |
| Physical progress | 72.09% | Motijheel–Kamalapur extension |
| Financial progress | 80.68% | Expenditure against the third revised allocation |

Do not merge these three percentages into one project-completion percentage. Financial progress is not physical progress. The 99.60% figure applies to the Uttara North–Motijheel section, not to the Kamalapur extension.

The same PDF also lists package-level physical progress on printed page 4. These percentages refer to separate work packages, not the whole MRT-6 line:

| Package | Description | Reported package progress |
|---|---|---:|
| CP-05 | 3.195 km viaduct and 3 elevated stations, Agargaon–Karwan Bazar | 100% |

| CP-07 | Electrical and mechanical systems procurement | 99.87% |
| CP-08 | Rolling stock and depot equipment procurement | 98.21% |

These are reported package progress values from the February 2026 RTHD update, not independent site certification or passenger-service outcomes. They must not be averaged with the section-level or financial percentages above.

The same February 2026 report gives:

- Original approved project total: **Tk 21,985.07 crore**.
- Third revised project total: **Tk 32,717.73 crore**.
- Third revised GoB share: **Tk 12,521.97 crore**.
- Third revised project-aid share: **Tk 20,195.76 crore**.
- Revised scheduled completion: **December 2028**.

The arithmetic sum of the annual reported-expenditure rows in the February report is **Tk 26,381.42 crore** through the report's stated periods, including the partial FY2025–26 row. It is not a complete FY2025–26 actual and should not be mistaken for an independent audited total.

**Currentness warning:** RTHD's mega-project index lists a Line-6 update dated **12 October 2026**. The quantitative progress metrics transcribed here are still from the older February 2026 PDF. The October file must be extracted before the panel is labelled as the latest status.

**Expected outputs/outcomes:** The approved project is intended to provide mass-transit infrastructure to improve efficient, safe and environmentally preferable public transport in Dhaka. The cited progress report supplies construction/financial measures, but not independently measured passenger outcomes, journey-time savings, emissions reductions, fare affordability or safety improvements. Those outcome fields remain unknown until suitable operational evidence is added.

## 8. Map location and 3D-model rules

### Project-location precision

The atlas uses the following location levels:

1. **Exact point:** only when an official source gives a usable project/site coordinate or surveyed facility point.
2. **Route geometry:** only when an official alignment or valid authoritative route dataset is available.
3. **Upazila/district area:** when the source names the administrative area but does not give a point.
4. **Not geocoded:** when source text is too broad, conflicting, or unclear.

A district-centre dot must not be used as a substitute for a project coordinate. If a project spans multiple districts or roads, show the listed administrative areas or source-supported corridor and explain the precision. The map must not place a contractor's office at the project site.

### 3D model policy

- Mega-project cards may include a 3D conceptual schematic when official engineering/BIM files are not public.
- Every schematic must carry a visible **Conceptual — not engineering/as-built geometry** label.
- Only approved design drawings, official BIM, surveyed station/road geometry and as-built records may be described as actual project models.
- Progress bars are created only from dated source percentages with a clearly named metric and project section.
- Physical progress and financial progress have separate bars.
- If no dated percentage exists, display **Progress data not published in the imported source** rather than a guessed 0%, 50% or 100%.
- Model animation must not imply that a project has been built or completed. Use an explicit timeline only when dated records exist.

## 9. Intended outcomes versus measured outcomes

Every project record should keep four separate fields:

- **Planned output:** what the approved project says it intends to build or deliver.
- **Reported output/progress:** what the implementing agency states has been done, with reporting date and source.
- **Measured outcome:** service/output evidence such as passenger usage, travel time, access, capacity, reliability, road condition or operational availability.
- **Independent verification:** independent site/engineering/audit or outcome evaluation, if one exists.

A completed bridge, road or metro structure is an output, not by itself proof of the intended social/economic outcome. A project allocation or award is not outcome evidence. National targets in the budget speech must not be presented as achieved results.
