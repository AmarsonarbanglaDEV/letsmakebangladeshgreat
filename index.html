Our standard

Evidence before conclusions.

This is an independent public-interest information project. Its job is to make public records easier to understand—not to decide guilt or produce political verdicts.

  • Allocation is not expenditure. An approved budget does not prove money was spent.
  • Transfer is not project payment. Money moving to an institution or region does not prove a specific asset received it.
  • Delay is not proof of wrongdoing. Show the schedule, source and documented reason.
  • Audit observation is not a conviction. Include official responses and follow-up status.
  • Unknown stays unknown. Never fill missing figures with guesses.
